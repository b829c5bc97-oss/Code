Quick start

From payment to snow on your screen takes about 2 minutes.

  1. Download. After paying, you get an email with your activation key and a link to DeskCrew.zip. Click the link to download it.
  2. Open. Open your Downloads folder, double-click DeskCrew.zip, then double-click DeskCrew. If your Mac says it "can't be opened" or "could not verify" it, see the first fix below.
  3. Move to Applications. DeskCrew asks to move itself into your Applications folder. Click Move to Applications: no dragging needed.
  4. Activate and set up. Paste your key and press Activate. Then name your village, name your 3 elves and pick a wallpaper. Snow starts falling straight away.

That's it. DeskCrew now starts by itself whenever your Mac turns on. You'll find it as a small ❄ snowflake in the menu bar at the top right of your screen.

Works on: any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or Intel, running macOS 12 Monterey or newer.

Your activation key

Your key is in the receipt email from Dodo Payments, sent the moment you paid. It's a long line of letters and numbers.

  • Entering it: DeskCrew asks for it the first time you open it. Copy the whole key from the email, paste it in, and press Activate. You need internet only for this moment; after that DeskCrew works offline.
  • Two Macs: one key works on 2 Macs, for example a work Mac and a home Mac.
  • Moving to a new Mac: on the old Mac, click ❄ → Settings → Move DeskCrew to another Mac. This frees up the key. Then install DeskCrew on the new Mac and activate it with the same key. If the old Mac is gone (sold, broken or lost), email us and we'll free the key for you.
  • Can't find the email? It comes from Dodo Payments, so search your inbox, spam and Promotions for "DeskCrew" or "Dodo". Still nothing? Email us with the address you paid with and we'll send your key again.

If the key doesn't work, the message under the box says why: see The key won't activate.

What happens when

  • Before 1 December: snow falls, piles up on your windows, and the site sign waits for the build to start.
  • 1–24 December: the first time you use your Mac each day, the machines arrive and build that day's part of Santa's Workshop. The elves then finish it by hand over about 10 minutes, and a wooden sign shows how far they've got.
  • Any time: machines pass by every so often, and rare surprises appear only while you're using your Mac. Click a surprise to catch it; it's added to your Spotter's log.
  • 1, 6, 13 and 20 December: the folders on your desktop get a snow cap and a sprig of holly or a bow.
  • 24 December, from 8 pm: the Christmas Eve film plays by itself, with sound, the first time you open your Mac (or straight away if you're already on it). It plays once more the first time you open your Mac on Christmas Day. Afterwards every window wears a garland with lights, and your folders get lights, bows and Santa hats, until the end of 26 December. Fireworks go off now and then.

DeskCrew hides itself while you use an app in full screen, and comes back when you leave it.

Fixes for common problems

"DeskCrew can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify DeskCrew"

  1. Find DeskCrew in your Downloads or Applications folder.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) DeskCrew and choose Open.
  3. Click Open in the box that appears. You only do this once.

Still blocked? Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to DeskCrew.

I don't see anything on my screen

  1. Look for the ❄ snowflake in the menu bar (top right). No snowflake? Open DeskCrew again from Applications.
  2. Click ❄. If you see Resume DeskCrew, click it.
  3. Leave any full-screen app: DeskCrew hides while one is open.
  4. Look along the bottom of your main screen; with two screens, the village is on the one with the menu bar.

The key won't activate

The message under the key box tells you which case it is:

  • "We couldn't find that key": copy it again from the email, the whole line, with no spaces before or after.
  • "This key is already on 2 Macs": free it on an old Mac (❄ → Settings → Move DeskCrew to another Mac), or email us.
  • "No internet": connect to Wi-Fi and press Activate again. It's only needed once.
  • "This key has been turned off": this happens after a refund. Email us if that's a mistake.

"DeskCrew wants to control Finder"

On the days your folders are decorated, macOS asks once whether DeskCrew may talk to Finder. DeskCrew only asks Finder where your desktop icons are, so it can put snow and bows in the right places. It never opens, reads or moves your files. Click OK to allow it, or Don't Allow: everything else keeps working. You can change your mind in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

My desktop uses Stacks: "DeskCrew would like to control this computer using accessibility features"

With Stacks (or Sort By) on, Finder can't tell apps where your icons are. So DeskCrew offers to look at the screen instead, the same way screen readers do. Click Open System Settings, then switch on DeskCrew under Accessibility. If macOS then asks whether DeskCrew may control "System Events", click OK. DeskCrew only checks where your icons sit. It never clicks, types or opens your files. If you'd rather not, your folders simply stay undecorated, never decorated in the wrong place. After a DeskCrew update, macOS may ask you to allow it once more.

Something on my screen won't click

DeskCrew lets every click through, except while Edit my village is on. Press Done at the top of the screen to leave edit mode.

Today's build didn't happen

The build plays the first time you use your Mac each day from 1 December, so it may have played while you weren't looking. Click ❄ → Watch today's build to see it again. Also check that your Mac's date and time are set automatically (System Settings → General → Date & Time).

No sound in the Christmas Eve show

Turn your Mac's volume up and check that ❄ → Settings → Sound is on. With headphones connected, the sound plays there.

The wallpaper didn't change

If you use several desktops (Spaces), macOS gives each its own wallpaper. Open DeskCrew → Wallpapers and press Set as my wallpaper again while on each desktop.

My Mac feels slower or the battery drains faster

Click ❄ → Settings and choose a lighter Snow amount and a smaller Village size. Pause DeskCrew during heavy work; it costs nothing while paused.

Settings, pausing and uninstalling

  • Start at login is on from the start, so DeskCrew comes back by itself after a restart. Turn it off in ❄ → Settings.
  • Pause: ❄ → Pause DeskCrew hides everything; Resume DeskCrew brings it back exactly as it was.
  • Quit: ❄ → Quit DeskCrew. It stays closed until you open it again or restart your Mac.
  • Uninstall: click ❄ → Settings → Move DeskCrew to another Mac (so your key is free for later), quit DeskCrew, then drag it from Applications to the Bin. Your old wallpaper comes back when you quit; if it doesn't, pick one in System Settings → Wallpaper.

Updating DeskCrew

When a newer DeskCrew is out, the snowflake ❄ menu shows Get the new DeskCrew and DeskCrew mentions it once on your screen. It never downloads anything by itself.

  1. Open the email from Dodo Payments and use the same download link as before. It always gives you the newest version.
  2. Quit DeskCrew (❄ → Quit DeskCrew), double-click the new DeskCrew.zip in Downloads and drag the new DeskCrew into Applications. Choose Replace.
  3. Open DeskCrew. Your village, names, key and settings are all kept.

Your village and crew names can be changed until 24 December at 9:00 am. After that they're locked in for the Christmas Eve film, which DeskCrew prepares on your Mac ahead of time so it plays smoothly at 8 pm.
