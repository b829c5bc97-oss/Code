Questions

Good to know

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or Intel, running macOS 12 Monterey or newer.

Will it slow my Mac down or get in the way?

No. DeskCrew draws a light layer along the bottom of the screen, lets every click through to your apps, and hides itself while you use an app in full screen. You can pause it any time from the ❄ menu.

What if I buy it after 1 December?

You'll start on that day's piece, with everything before it already built. Nothing is missed.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once for the Christmas 2026 season. Nothing renews.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Yes, one key works on 2 Macs. You can move it to a new Mac from the menu whenever you like.

What do you do with my data?

Nothing. There's no account and no tracking. Your key is checked with our payment provider when you activate. Everything else stays on your Mac. See the privacy policy.

What if it doesn't work on my Mac?

Email us within 14 days and we'll refund you in full. Most problems are fixed in a minute with the help guide.
