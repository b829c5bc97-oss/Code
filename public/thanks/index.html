Thank you for getting DeskCrew! In the next minute you'll get an email from Dodo Payments with your activation key and your download link. Can't see it? Check spam and Promotions, or search for "DeskCrew".

Set up in 2 minutes

  1. Download DeskCrew with the link in the email, then double-click DeskCrew.zip in your Downloads folder.
  2. Open DeskCrew and click Move to Applications when it asks.
  3. Paste your key from the email into the welcome window and press Activate.
  4. Name your village and your three elves, pick a wallpaper, and watch the snow start to fall.

If your Mac says it "can't be opened" or "could not verify" DeskCrew, right-click DeskCrew and choose Open. More fixes are in the help guide.
