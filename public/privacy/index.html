Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

DeskCrew is a small app made to be festive, not to collect data. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains exactly what leaves your Mac and what doesn't.

What we receive

  • When you buy: payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. Dodo collects your name, email, billing country and payment details to process the payment, send your receipt and your activation key, and meet tax rules. We see your name, email, country and order details in our Dodo account. We never see your full card details. Dodo's own privacy policy applies to the checkout.
  • When you activate: the app sends your activation key and a label made from your Mac's name (for example "Maya's MacBook (DeskCrew)") to Dodo Payments, so the key can be tied to up to 2 Macs. About once a day it checks that the key is still valid. Nothing else is sent.
  • When you email us: we receive whatever you choose to send, and use it only to help you.

What stays on your Mac

  • Window positions: DeskCrew reads where your windows are so snow can settle on top of them. It doesn't read what's inside your windows, and the positions never leave your Mac.
  • Desktop icons: on decoration days, DeskCrew asks Finder for the names and positions of the icons on your desktop so it can decorate them. It never opens, reads or moves your files, and this information never leaves your Mac.
  • Screen recording: only if you press Record during a rare surprise. The clip is saved to your Downloads folder and goes nowhere else.
  • Your settings (village name, elf names, pieces you placed, the Spotter's log) are stored in DeskCrew's settings file on your Mac.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. Our host (Netlify) keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the order information we hold (we may have to keep some records for tax law), or ask what we hold about you, by emailing coinwisekids@gmail.com. Uninstalling DeskCrew removes the app; its settings file is in your Library folder under Application Support → DeskCrew.

Children

DeskCrew is family-friendly, but purchases must be made by an adult.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, mention it on this site.
