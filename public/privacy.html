Privacy policy

Social HQ · Last updated 22 November 2026

Social HQ is a private tool used only by Pratham Bansal to plan, schedule and publish posts to his own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn, YouTube and Pinterest) and to see how those accounts are doing. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign up.

What Social HQ stores

  • The login keys that Meta, Google, LinkedIn and Pinterest issue when the owner connects his own accounts. They are stored encrypted and are used only to publish the owner's posts and read the owner's account statistics.
  • Basic account details returned by those platforms: account name, handle, profile picture and IDs.
  • The owner's scheduled posts, captions and the photos or videos he uploads for them.
  • Account statistics (followers, reach, views, interactions) for the owner's own accounts.
  • A security log of logins and changes, including IP address and browser type.

What Social HQ does not do

  • It does not sell, share or rent any data to anyone.
  • It does not collect data about other people's accounts, and it does not use data for advertising.
  • It does not read private messages.

Google user data (YouTube)

Social HQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube access is used only to upload the owner's videos to his own channels and to show his channels' statistics. Social HQ uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where data is kept

The site runs on Vercel (hosting) and data is kept in Supabase (database and file storage). Platform login keys are encrypted with a key held only by the site.

Removing access and deleting data

Any connected account can be removed in Social HQ (Accounts → Remove), which deletes its stored login key and statistics. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform itself: Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Facebook/Instagram (Settings → Business integrations), LinkedIn (Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services) or Pinterest (Settings → Apps). To ask for deletion of all data, email info.liftline@gmail.com; it is completed within 30 days.
